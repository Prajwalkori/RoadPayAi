Process Flow

How the System Works

From camera photo capture to online payment, here is the lifecycle flow of a traffic ticket in RoadPay AI.

01

Evidence Capture

Traffic cameras or officers upload photos or videos of road safety violations.

02

AI Violation Detection

AI vision models analyze the captured photo to identify the specific safety violation.

03

License Plate OCR

The system automatically reads the license plate number from the photo.

04

Owner Lookup

The system matches the license plate with the vehicle registry to retrieve owner details.

05

Ticket Generation

A formal PDF ticket is compiled with proof photos, safety guidelines, and payment links.

06

Automatic Alerts

The driver receives an email notification with a secure link to the safety module.

07

Safety Quiz

The owner learns about safety rules and takes a quick quiz about their specific violation.

08

Fine Discount

Scoring at least 2/3 correct on the quiz applies an instant 20% discount on the fine.

09

Secure Settle

The owner pays the discounted fine online, updating the database status to PAID instantly.
